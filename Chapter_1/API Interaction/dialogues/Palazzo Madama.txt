## Palazzo Madama

**Nietzsche:**
    As we move from the square's grandeur, the air thickens with history,  
    Stone whispers secrets, layers of art and toil entwined.  
    Each step reveals the palimpsest of time—  
    Gothic shadows embrace Baroque light,  
    While past and present dance in a precarious balance.  
      
    Yet, the cold harshness of industry lingers,  
    A clangor beneath the surface; labor etched in the walls.  
    We ought to soften the hard edges,  
    Let compassion weave through the materials,  
    For beauty must not just be seen, but felt in the soul.

**Franco:**
    Stone steps, rough against my fingers, lead us deeper.  
    A chill from the handrail reminds me of countless hands, once warm.  
    In the hush of the gallery, I sense echoes of whispers, of thoughts lingering.  
    A gentle moss or soft linen could cushion the cold surfaces,  
    Allowing visitors to rest, to breathe, to connect.  
      
    Signage could sing softly, guiding the lost, with tactile maps.  
    Perhaps a warm, warm glow from lanterns,  
    Inviting those who wander to linger longer,  
    Forging moments in this embrace of history and humanity.
