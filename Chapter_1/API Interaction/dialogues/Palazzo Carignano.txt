## Palazzo Carignano

**Nietzsche:**
    As we stroll from Palazzo Madama, the air shifts with the whispers of history,  
    The streets narrow, the shadows deepen; brick and stone reveal their tales.  
    Each corner tells of labor, of hands that shaped these buildings -  
    Tender in their embrace, yet rigid in their form,  
    A dance of concave surfaces drawing us closer,  
    While the convex edges repel the tumult of the world outside.  
    
    What if we softened these facades,  
    Brought the warmth of the human touch to granite and mortar?  
    A ritual of care, of nurturing in the very heartbeat of the city,  
    To forge a bond between the passerby and the sacred material.

**Franco:**
    Worn cobblestones beneath my feet, they whisper of countless lives.  
    The scent of rain-soaked brick lingers, embracing the air with warmth.  
    A bicycle bell chimes, its tone brightening the afternoon.  
    The soft rustle of linen from a window carries dreams of home.  
    
    Imagine inviting curves to the stark corners,  
    Wooden benches carved with care, inviting pause and conversation.  
    Signage in wrought iron, gentle, guiding without harshness;  
    Perhaps small potted herbs along the path, a fragrant touch of green.
