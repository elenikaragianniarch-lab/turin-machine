## Piazza CLN

**Nietzsche:**
    As we leave the square—its harsh symmetry tightens around us—the air grows thick with the murmurs of the city. The stone facades, austere like sentinels, seem to draw closer, pressing against our thoughts. 
    
    With each step, the laughter of life mingles with the echo of industry; the clanking of craftsman's tools lingers in the shadows. One longs for softer forms, for curves to cradle the sharpness of existence. 
    
    Oh, how these streets demand a gentler touch—a fountain adorned with blossoms, benches to gather the weary, the laughter of children in these echoing corners. Let us nurture the spirit here, so that it may breathe amidst the severity.

**Franco:**
    Each footfall resonates against the cobblestones, a sharp rhythm of industry; the scent of polished wood mingles with a hint of oil from nearby workshops. The air is thick, carrying whispers from the fountain, a soft lull amid the rigid facades.
    
    Piazza CLN could cradle warmth with rounded benches, crafted from smooth, polished stone—encouraging gatherings, breath, and laughter. Faintly scented flowers could spill from terracotta pots, leading the eye and heart toward the water's edge.
    
    A gentle fountain—its murmur softened by cascading petals—could invite pause, drawing both the weary and curious into its embrace. Signage could be tactile, with raised letters and warm materials, guiding the blind and sighted alike through the square's stern lines.
